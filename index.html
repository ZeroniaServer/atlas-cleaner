What does this site do?
It prepares Minecraft 26.1-26.3 world ZIPs by pruning fully empty chunks and removing 0kb region files, poi, player, generated, scoreboards, level.dat_old, and session.lock as well as hidden or unneeded documentation files.

Allows selecting dimensions and datapacks to remove. And packages and cleans resourcepacks with similar metadata rules.
